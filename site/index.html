quillier Source code

A quiet place to write your book —
and nothing else.

Quillier is a free writing app for novels and other long work. It keeps your book on your computer in one file, keeps track of who’s in it, and stays out of your way.

Download for Mac

Also for Intel Macs, Windows and Linux.

Opening it for the first time on Windows

If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. That happens with new apps that aren’t code-signed yet.

Opening it for the first time on Linux

Make the AppImage executable (in its file properties, or with chmod +x), then double-click it.

Your story bible, kept up to date

Give each character and place an entry, with aliases and as many notes as you like. As you write, Quillier finds them in your chapters on its own, and shows who appears where, who shares scenes and who’s gone quiet.

Characters, places and scenes
Each entry gets a name, aliases, a few short fields and room for as many notes as you want. Scene notes attach to the scenes in your chapters, so nothing gets described twice.
Tracked from your text, not tags
You never mark anything up. Quillier finds the names itself, so pasted and imported chapters count too, and exports stay clean. Aliases count as well: “Edith”, “Gran” and “Edith Lind” are one person. Names that are also ordinary words, like Hope or May, can be left out.
Names finish themselves
Type the first letters of a name and the rest appears in gray. Tab takes it; keep typing to ignore it. …and handed the ledger to EdithTab
A cast list for every chapter
Each chapter and scene shows who’s in it and where it takes place, worked out from the text.
Relationships
Link two characters, label the link and keep notes on it. See the whole cast as a map, or as a grid once it gets big.
Threads you’ve dropped
When someone hasn’t appeared for a while, the dashboard says so. Every mention is a click away.
All on your computer
Tracking is plain name-matching that runs on your machine, and it’s quick even on a 100,000-word draft. No AI reads your book.

Made for a whole book

Chapters, in order
Number, reorder and rename chapters right in the sidebar. Mark each one not started, in progress, drafted, revised or done.
Drafts you can branch
Copy the whole manuscript into a new draft before a big rewrite, and switch between drafts whenever you like.
An outline and a notebook
Plan in outline sections beside the manuscript, and keep research and loose ideas on a free-form notes page.
Notes on the text
Select a passage and leave yourself a note. It shows in the side panel and never ends up in your text, so exports come out clean.
Punctuation that fixes itself
Quotes curl, two hyphens become an em dash, and three dots become an ellipsis. Type 1/2 or (c) and it offers ½ or ©.
Focus mode and dark mode
Hide everything but the page. Dark mode has a pure black setting for OLED screens.
Export for submission
Standard manuscript format as a PDF for agents and editors, an EPUB ebook that passes the W3C’s checker, a book-style PDF, HTML or Markdown.

Your book is one file

Each book is a single .quill file, saved wherever you choose. Inside, it’s plain JSON with every chapter in Markdown, so your words are never locked in, with or without Quillier.

It saves as you type. Saves can’t leave half a file behind if your computer crashes, and a backup copy is kept every ten minutes while you write.

Nothing you write leaves your computer. There’s no account and no analytics. The only time Quillier goes online is to check for a new version, and you can turn that off in Settings.

Free and open source

Quillier costs nothing, with no subscription and nothing to sign in to. The code is MIT-licensed and on GitHub, where you can read it, report a bug or suggest an idea.
