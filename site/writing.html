Folders and book parts

File chapters in folders, as deep as you like; the numbers run straight through. A folder marked as a book part opens with a “Part One” page in every export. Click a status to change it.

An outline linked to the book

Start blank, or from one of five structures, each beat explained and ready to write over. Outline folders can appear in the manuscript, and a chapter linked to its beats shows their notes as you write.

Forty years of it, and not one word about the man who rowed out in the winter of 1961 and never rowed back.

Too on the nose? Maybe hold this back until the storm.

Punctuation that fixes itself

Quotes curl, two hyphens become an em dash and three dots an ellipsis. Fractions and symbols are offered as you type.

  2. 2Outside, the tide had gone out further…866

Scenes inside chapters

Type three asterisks to break a chapter into scenes. Each one is listed with its opening words and length, a click away.
