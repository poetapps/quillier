Saved as you go

Save a version before a big change, named if you like. Quillier also keeps one each time you sit down to write.

Restored “Before cutting the flashback”. What you had is saved as “Before restoring”.Undo

Nothing is ever lost

Restoring a version saves what you had first. Deleted takes go to the trash, and every step can be undone.

Drafts you can branch

Copy the whole manuscript into a new draft before a big rewrite, and switch between drafts whenever you like.

One change at a time

Take back a single cut, or keep it, right from the margin, instead of restoring the whole chapter.

Words, not lines

Changes are marked word by word inside each paragraph, the way an editor would, and a moved paragraph shows as moved.

Side by side, or only what changed

Put the two texts next to each other, or fold away every paragraph that didn’t change.

Takes compare too

Any two takes of a chapter can be compared the same way, and either one put in the book.

Tidy on its own

Automatic versions older than a few days thin out to one a day. The ones you name stay until you delete them.

In the same file

Takes and versions are kept in the book’s .quill file, so they go wherever it goes.
